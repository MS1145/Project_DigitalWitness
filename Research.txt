  2. Emerging Alternatives

  A. Video Vision Transformers (ViViT / TimeSformer)

  - Captures long-range temporal dependencies better than LSTM
  - TimeSformer: 82.2% on Kinetics-400
  - No need for separate CNN feature extraction
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15691

  B. Skeleton-Based GCN (ST-GCN / AGCN)

  - Uses pose keypoints instead of raw video
  - Privacy-preserving (no facial features)
  - Lighter computation
  - https://www.ieice.org/publications/conference-FIT-DVDs/FIT2024/data/ht  ml/program/pdf/H-041.pdf

  C. Multi-Component Systems

  Recent research integrates 4 components achieving 95% overall accuracy: 
  1. Person detection (YOLO)
  2. Activity recognition (CNN-LSTM)
  3. Product detection (YOLO)
  4. Person re-identification (Siamese Networks)

  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11760-025-04543-4
